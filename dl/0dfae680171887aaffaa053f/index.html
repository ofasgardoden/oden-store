The formulas, in plain English

outcome: win = 1, loss = 0. Leave open trades blank — they're excluded until resolved.

Brier score: brier = 2 × (p_win/100 − outcome)²

  • 0.00 — perfect call (said 100% and won, or 0% and it failed)
  • 0.33 — coin-flip level (no better than chance)
  • < 0.15 — sharp; you know your edge
  • 0.98 — said 70% and lost: false confidence, punished

Weekly calibration: average the Brier scores of all resolved rows in the window. Track that number — falling average = real improvement. Compare it to your win-rate: a flat win-rate with a falling Brier means you're learning where your edge lives.

The one rule: fill p_win_pct before the trade resolves. No hindsight edits. The discipline is the product.
