The Calibration Trading Journal

Grades your predictions, not your excuses. Commit to a probability before the trade — get scored after.

What it does

  • Commit: log thesis, entry/target/stop, horizon — and your honest win probability — before resolution
  • Score: every resolved trade gets a brier = 2 × (p − outcome)²; 0.33 is coin-flip, under 0.15 is sharp
  • Calibrate: weekly block exposes your overconfidence gap (most traders run 15–25 points hot)
  • No hindsight edits: pre-registration is baked into the format — the standard audit-grade desks use
  • Honest examples included: three worked rows, including a loss scored at 0.98 — we show misses, not just wins

How it works

1
Before entry, fill one row: thesis, prices, horizon, and your p(win) as a percentage. No row, no trade.
2
When the trade resolves, mark the outcome. The Brier formula scores how good your probability was — not just P&L.
3
Weekly: average your Brier scores. Watch the number fall. That number is your edge, measured.

Why Brier?

It's the scoring rule meteorologists and forecast labs use — it punishes false confidence harder than honest uncertainty. A trader who says 70% and wins feels great; Brier tells you whether that 70% was real or noise. If you can't measure calibration, you can't improve it.
